## 1. **How do I use it?**

The dashboard is interactive and allows you to filter the data.

**Available Filter:**

* **Mental Health Status** (Anxiety, Depression, Panic Attack, No Mental Health Challenges)

<img width="1480" height="332" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/2d18dfc3-a4d1-4c67-aec9-2cdda62e8eb5" />


When you select a mental health status, the dashboard updates to show:

* **Courses** → which academic programs are most associated with that condition
* **Gender** → which gender is more impacted
* **Age Group** → which age groups are linked to that condition
* **CGPA** → what GPA ranges are associated with that condition

All visualizations update automatically when the filter changes.

---

## 2. **Why does it matter?**

Mental health challenges such as **anxiety, depression, and panic attacks** have a profound impact on students’ learning, performance, and well-being. By visualizing the moderators linked to these conditions, institutions can better understand **what factors are driving mental health differences** among students and develop targeted support systems.

---

## 3. **What is the key question?**

* **What moderators (course, gender, age, GPA) contribute to or are associated with each mental health status?**

---

## 4. **What data is behind it?**

The dataset includes student records with the following key attributes:

* **Mental Health Status** (Anxiety, Depression, Panic Attack, No Challenges)
* **Course** of study
* **Gender**
* **Age**
* **CGPA** (Cumulative Grade Point Average)

This data is used to reveal **patterns and moderators** of mental health differences across student groups.

---

## 5. **What does it show?**

By filtering for each mental health status, users can explore:

* Which **courses** have more students experiencing anxiety, depression, or panic attacks
* Which **gender** is more affected by each condition
* Which **age groups** show higher prevalence of mental health challenges
* Which **GPA ranges** are most associated with different mental health statuses

---

## 6. **Who is it for?**

This dashboard is designed for:

* **University administrators** — to include mental health considerations in planning and policy
* **Counselors and psychologists** — to identify moderators that place students at risk
* **Faculty and program coordinators** — to understand course-specific mental health trends
* **Policy makers and researchers** — to explore the links between academics and mental health outcomes

---

## 7. **What’s next?**

This Tableau dashboard is a **visual decision-support tool** that helps universities:

* Detect moderators linked to mental health conditions
* Compare patterns across different statuses (e.g., Anxiety vs. Depression)
* Create evidence-based strategies for improving **student mental health and academic success**
